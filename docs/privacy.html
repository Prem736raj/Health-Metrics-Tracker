Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Health Metrics Tracker ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information.

Summary: Your personal health metrics (such as BMI, Blood Pressure, WHR, Water Intake, and BSA) are stored locally on your device by default. We do not sell your personal data or share your health records with third-party advertisers.

1. Information We Collect

  • Health & Wellness Data: Profile details and an optional profile photo, height, age, weight and goals, blood pressure, waist/hip measurements, hydration, food/calorie and nutrient entries, calculator inputs/results, and notes that you choose to record. These records and settings are stored locally in app-private Room, DataStore or SharedPreferences storage. The app does not claim database-level encryption.
  • Health Connect Data (Optional): If you grant permission, the app requests separate read-only access to steps (android.permission.health.READ_STEPS) and weight (android.permission.health.READ_WEIGHT). Access can be revoked in Health Connect. Raw provider records are not automatically transmitted to third-party servers.
  • Optional Product Analytics: Product-usage analytics are off by default. If you enable the setting, the app may send allowlisted events such as a feature being opened or a reminder being enabled through Firebase Analytics. Events do not include health measurements, notes, profile details, names, contact details, or direct identifiers. Firebase may still process standard pseudonymous app/device information needed to provide analytics.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To provide informational body and wellness estimates (BMI, Body Surface Area, WHR, etc.) using the selected equations and reference rules.
  • To track your hydration, streak milestones, and wellness habits over time.
  • To understand aggregate feature use and improve navigation only when optional product analytics is enabled.

3. Data Storage & Security

Core health records remain in the application's private Android sandbox on your device. Automatic Android backups are disabled (android:allowBackup="false"). When you choose to export a report or data file, the selected content is handed to the Android share/files system and may be stored by the destination you choose. The app does not currently offer automatic cloud backup or cross-device restore.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Firebase Analytics (optional): Receives only the allowlisted, non-health product events described above when you turn on Settings > Privacy > Share optional product usage. Collection is disabled by default and can be turned off at any time.

5. Medical Disclaimer

Health Metrics Tracker is an educational wellness tool and is not a medical device. Calculations and informational summaries are estimates and do not constitute medical diagnosis, treatment, or prescription.

6. Data Deletion & Rights

You can delete the application's local data under Settings > Data Management > Clear All Data, or by uninstalling the application. This does not delete source records in Health Connect, copies you exported to other apps, or information already processed by third-party services. Turning off optional analytics stops future analytics collection; it is not a server-side deletion request. Contact the address below for questions about information already sent. Third-party processing and retention must be considered separately from on-device deletion.
