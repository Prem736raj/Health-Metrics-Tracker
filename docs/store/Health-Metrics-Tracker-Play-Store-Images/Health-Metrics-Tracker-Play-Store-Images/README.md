# Health Metrics Tracker — Play Store screenshot pack

12 polished phone screenshots, plus the 12 original app captures. The first eight form a suggested upload sequence; four additional images provide alternatives. This task started on 9 October and finished on 10 October 2026, Asia/Kolkata.

## Files to use

- `recommended-8/`: the eight suggested phone-listing PNGs, numbered in upload order.
- `polished/`: all twelve finished images at full resolution.
- `originals/`: all twelve clean app captures, for an interface-only listing if preferred.
- `preview.jpg`: an overview of all twelve finished images; do not upload this contact sheet as a screenshot.
- `gallery.html`: a local gallery with links to each full-resolution PNG and its original capture.
- `alt-text.csv`: accessible descriptions to copy into the listing.
- `validation.json`: dimensions, file formats, hashes, uniqueness checks and original pixel-preservation checks.
- `screens.json`: editable headings, supporting text, routes and accessible descriptions.
- `background-artwork.png`: the supporting artwork used behind the app captures.

## Export and design details

Every finished screenshot is a 1080 × 1920 portrait, 24-bit RGB PNG without transparency. The marketing copy sits above the capture and takes less than 20% of the image height. The app capture is proportionally reduced to 900 × 1600 and placed in a simple card with a subtle border and shadow. The entire captured screen is included, with only a small corner mask around the card. No phone hardware, store badge, ranking, price promotion or invented app feature was added.

The originals preserve decoded RGB pixels exactly. The finished images change presentation around the screenshot; they do not redraw, retouch or synthesize the application interface, readings or chart contents. The 9:41 clock and cleaned notifications use Android System UI demo mode on the emulator.

## Capture provenance and sample entries

Captures come from the real Health Metrics Tracker 1.0.1 minified release QA APK, installed on the dedicated rooted API 34 emulator at 1080 × 1920 with 360 dpi and normal font size. This QA copy is signed with an Android debug certificate. It is not the final Google Play-signed distribution artifact; compare the listing against the distributed version before publishing.

The dedicated emulator already contained synthetic QA measurements. Additional clearly marked `Sample listing data` fixtures were inserted into the emulator's real Room database for weight, water and blood pressure, so the actual app rendered its summaries and charts. Synthetic food entries were added using the app's quick-add presets. The BMI example was calculated through the app with age 28, male, weight 71.2 kg and height 175 cm; its displayed estimate is 23.2. No real person's health records were used. Database backups remain locally in `scratch/listing-sample-data/` and are not distributed in the artwork ZIP.

The application source was not changed for these screenshots. The capture and artwork scripts live in `tools/capture_listing.py`, `tools/seed_listing_sample_data.py` and `tools/build_listing_artwork.mjs`.
